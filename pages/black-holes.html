Black Holes

A black hole is a region of space where gravity is so strong that even light cannot escape from it.

Gravity Extremely Strong
Event Horizon Boundary of No Return
Space Cosmic Object

What Is a Black Hole?

A black hole forms when a large amount of matter becomes compressed into a very small region, creating extremely strong gravity.

Event Horizon

The event horizon is the boundary around a black hole. Once something crosses this boundary, it cannot escape back to the outside according to current physics.

How Are Black Holes Formed?

Some black holes form when massive stars collapse after using up their nuclear fuel. Supermassive black holes exist at the centres of many galaxies.

Important Facts

  • Black holes do not produce light directly.
  • Scientists detect them through their effects on nearby matter.
  • Some black holes are formed from collapsing stars.
  • The Milky Way contains a supermassive black hole at its centre.
