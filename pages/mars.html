Mars

Mars is the fourth planet from the Sun and is commonly called the Red Planet.

4th Planet from the Sun
2 Moons Phobos and Deimos
Red Planet Iron-rich Surface

Basic Information

Planet Number: Fourth from the Sun

Natural Satellites: Phobos and Deimos

Day Length: About 24 hours and 37 minutes

Year Length: About 687 Earth days

Atmosphere: Mostly carbon dioxide

Why Is Mars Red?

Mars appears red because iron minerals in its soil have reacted with oxygen and formed iron oxides.

Interesting Facts

  • Mars has two small moons.
  • It contains Olympus Mons, a huge volcano.
  • Mars has polar ice caps.
  • Scientists study Mars for signs of ancient water.
