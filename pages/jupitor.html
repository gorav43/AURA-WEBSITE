Jupiter

Jupiter is the largest planet in our Solar System and is a gas giant.

1st Largest Planet
Gas Giant Planet Type
Great Red Spot Huge Storm

Basic Information

Planet Number: Fifth from the Sun

Type: Gas giant

Largest Feature: Great Red Spot

Day Length: About 10 hours

Year Length: About 12 Earth years

Great Red Spot

The Great Red Spot is a giant storm in Jupiter's atmosphere. It has existed for many years and is larger than Earth.

Interesting Facts

  • Jupiter is the largest planet.
  • It has a very strong magnetic field.
  • Jupiter has many moons.
  • It is mainly made of hydrogen and helium.
