Stars and Galaxies

Stars are huge balls of hot gas, while galaxies are enormous groups of stars, gas, dust and dark matter.

What Is a Star?

A star is a massive, glowing sphere of plasma. Stars produce energy through nuclear fusion in their cores.

Our Sun

The Sun is the star at the centre of our Solar System. It provides the light and heat required for life on Earth.

Type: G-type main-sequence star

Location: Centre of the Solar System

What Is a Galaxy?

A galaxy is a large system containing stars, planets, gas, dust and dark matter held together by gravity.

Our Galaxy: Milky Way

Interesting Facts

  • Stars have different sizes, colours and temperatures.
  • The Sun is an average-sized star.
  • The Milky Way contains billions of stars.
  • Some galaxies contain trillions of stars.
