Maharana Pratap

Maharana Pratap was the ruler of Mewar, remembered for his courage, independence and resistance.

9 May 1540 Date of Birth
19 Jan 1597 Date of Death
Kumbhalgarh Birth Place

Personal Information

Full Name: Maharana Pratap Singh Sisodia

Date of Birth: 9 May 1540

Birth Place: Kumbhalgarh Fort, Rajasthan, India

Date of Death: 19 January 1597

Death Place: Chavand, Rajasthan, India

Father: Maharana Udai Singh II

Mother: Maharani Jaiwanta Bai

Kingdom: Mewar

Maharana Pratap is remembered as a symbol of courage, self-respect and determination.

Early Life

Maharana Pratap was born at Kumbhalgarh Fort in present-day Rajasthan. He belonged to the Sisodia Rajput dynasty of Mewar.

Battle of Haldighati

The Battle of Haldighati was fought in 1576 between the forces of Maharana Pratap and the Mughal army led by Man Singh I.

Important Facts

  • He was the ruler of Mewar.
  • His famous horse was Chetak.
  • He fought to protect the independence of Mewar.
  • His son was Amar Singh I.
