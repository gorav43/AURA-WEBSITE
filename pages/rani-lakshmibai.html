Rani Lakshmibai

Rani Lakshmibai of Jhansi was one of the prominent leaders of the Indian Rebellion of 1857.

19 Nov 1828 Date of Birth
18 Jun 1858 Date of Death
Varanasi Birth Place

Personal Information

Birth Name: Manikarnika Tambe

Popular Name: Rani Lakshmibai

Date of Birth: 19 November 1828

Birth Place: Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh, India

Date of Death: 18 June 1858

Death Place: Near Kotah-ki-Serai, Gwalior region

Kingdom: Jhansi

Husband: Maharaja Gangadhar Rao

Rani Lakshmibai became a major symbol of courage and resistance during the Revolt of 1857.

Early Life

Rani Lakshmibai was born as Manikarnika Tambe in Varanasi. She was popularly known as Manu during her childhood.

Role in the Revolt of 1857

She played a major role in defending Jhansi and later participated in military resistance against British forces.

Important Facts

  • She was the queen of Jhansi.
  • Her childhood name was Manikarnika.
  • She became an important figure of 1857.
  • Her son was Damodar Rao.
