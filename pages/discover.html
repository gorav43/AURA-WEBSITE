HISTORY

The Great Pyramids of Egypt

The Great Pyramids of Egypt are one of the most famous wonders of the ancient world. They were built thousands of years ago as tombs for Egyptian kings called pharaohs.

The Great Pyramid of Giza was built for Pharaoh Khufu. It was made from millions of stone blocks and shows the advanced engineering, mathematics and planning skills of ancient Egyptians.

Did You Know?

The Great Pyramid was the tallest human-made structure in the world for thousands of years.

SCIENCE

How Do Black Holes Work?

A black hole is a region of space where gravity is so strong that nothing, not even light, can escape from it.

Black holes can form when a very massive star reaches the end of its life and collapses under its own gravity. The boundary around a black hole is called the event horizon.

Did You Know?

Scientists cannot directly see a black hole, but they can observe its effect on nearby stars, gas and light.

Interesting Facts About Earth

Earth is the only known planet with life. It has oceans, mountains, forests, deserts and a protective atmosphere.

Earth takes approximately 24 hours to complete one rotation on its axis. It takes about 365 days to complete one revolution around the Sun.

Did You Know?

Around 71% of Earth's surface is covered by water, but most of that water is salty ocean water.
