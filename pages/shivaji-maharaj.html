Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj

Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj was the founder of the Maratha Empire and an important ruler in Indian history.

19 Feb 1630 Date of Birth
3 Apr 1680 Date of Death
Shivneri Fort Birth Place

Personal Information

Full Name: Shivaji Shahaji Bhonsale

Date of Birth: 19 February 1630

Birth Place: Shivneri Fort, Maharashtra region, India

Date of Death: 3 April 1680

Death Place: Raigad Fort, Maharashtra, India

Father: Shahaji Bhonsale

Mother: Jijabai

Empire: Maratha Empire

Shivaji Maharaj is remembered for his administration, fort management, naval strategy and the idea of Swarajya.

Early Life

Shivaji Maharaj was born at Shivneri Fort. His mother Jijabai played an important role in his upbringing and values.

Coronation

Shivaji Maharaj was crowned Chhatrapati at Raigad Fort in 1674.

Important Facts

  • Founder of the Maratha Empire.
  • Known for the concept of Hindavi Swarajya.
  • Built and managed several important forts.
  • Developed a strong naval presence.
