Earth

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only known planet to support life.

3rd Planet from the Sun
1 Moon Natural Satellite
365 Days One Year

Basic Information

Planet Number: Third from the Sun

Natural Satellite: Moon

Rotation Period: About 24 hours

Revolution Period: About 365.25 days

Atmosphere: Nitrogen and oxygen rich

Why Is Earth Special?

Earth has liquid water, a suitable atmosphere and conditions that support a wide variety of life forms.

Interesting Facts

  • Earth is known as the Blue Planet.
  • About 71% of its surface is covered by water.
  • Earth has one natural satellite, the Moon.
  • It has a protective magnetic field.
